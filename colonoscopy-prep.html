      Friday, October 2 — Procedure day

      NO SOLID FOOD. Clear liquids are allowed only until 5:45 AM. After that, nothing by mouth.

        Approved clear liquids

        ✓ OK to drink

        • Gatorade
        • Broth
        • Water
        • Black coffee, clear tea
        • Clear fruit juices
        • Jell-O, popsicles
        • Soda pop, Kool-Aid, Crystal Light
        • Any other clear liquid drink

        ✗ Avoid

        • Any solid food
        • Milk or cream
        • Protein drinks or Ensure-type shakes
        • Anything red
        • Alcohol
        • Fibrous or pulpy liquids
        • Nicotine in any form

        Drink at least 8 oz of clear liquid every hour while you're awake on the day before.

        Medications. Do not stop prescription medications unless the office tells you to. Do not take Bisacodyl (Dulcolax) if you are over 70 or have diarrhea. GLP-1 users (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Saxenda, etc.): skip your GLP-1 injection for 10 days before the procedure. If you take a GLP-1, opioid pain medicine, or have constipation, take Bisacodyl 10 mg in the evening on both of the two days before your colonoscopy.
