Get a library card

It’s free for anyone who lives, works, goes to school, or owns property in King County.

  1. 1Bring a photo ID and proof of your address to any service desk.
  2. 2A staff member will print your card and set your PIN with you.
  3. 3Come back here and sign in with the number under the barcode.
