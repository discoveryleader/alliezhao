What's wrong with it now

Nine defects, in the order they'd bite a user. The options below cite these by number.

  1. The stacked bar's segments physically overlap. Late sits at x 269–314, Total at x 254–336 — the yellow is drawn inside the red. What survives is 220px green, 44px yellow, 22px red, which is 77 / 15 / 8, not the 80 / 17 printed on it. Layers 5648:20037–39. The third segment is named "Total", which is not a category.
  2. Two different measures both read "80%". The ring shows tasks completed; the bar shows punctuality. They collide on the same number with nothing to tell them apart.
  3. The red segment has no label and no legend. Only "On time" is named. The reader has to guess what yellow and red mean, and 80 + 17 leaves 3% unaccounted for.
  4. "17%" is white on #FFCC4D — about 1.6:1. Well under the 4.5:1 floor for text that size. It is effectively invisible.
  5. The donut carries no number, and the sentence beneath repeats it. A two-slice donut plus "8 of 10 tasks completed" says one thing twice and shows it once.
  6. Three type families in a 351px card. Inter Bold for the sentence, Poppins SemiBold for the stats, Open Sans for the bar. The system only calls for two.
  7. Numbers are centred with hardcoded spaces. The stat values are literally "   5" and "    70", so a 3-digit coin total will sit off-centre.
  8. No timeframe anywhere on the card. "whole week" lives in a layer name; the user never sees it.
  9. Stock alert red on a card aimed at kids with ADHD. #EA2A2A in a 20px block reads as a penalty, and "See All" is a 57×18 target — well under 44px.

The three directions

They differ in how much they're willing to change, not in polish. Pick by appetite.

1

Nothing moves conceptually. Every defect gets fixed in place, so it can ship this sprint without a spec conversation.

What changes

  • The ring holds its own value — 9/10 done — so it no longer needs a sentence under it, and it can't be confused with the punctuality figure. 25
  • Three real segments that sum to 100, at 12px with 2px gaps instead of one 20px block, each named in a legend with its value. 139
  • Status colours re-cut for this surface — #12805C / #C9861F / #B84A43. They clear the CVD separation and lightness-band checks against #C4E3F5, and every value is written out rather than relying on the fill. 49
  • Poppins and Open Sans only. Stats become labelled tiles with a week-on-week delta, so "70 coins" finally means something. 67
  • "This week" is on the card; "See all" is a 44px target. 89

What it costs

  • Needs last week's totals for the deltas. Without them, drop the +2 / +15 chips and nothing else changes.
  • Still four measures in one card — denser than either alternative.
2

One story — a headline and a trend

The card currently gives four measures equal weight, so none of them lands. This picks the one that matters, makes it the hero, and spends the freed space on the thing the panel can't show today: whether it's improving.

What changes

  • The donut goes. A two-slice ring with no number in it was doing less work than the sentence beside it. 5
  • One hero figure at 46px, read in about a second, with the comparison the card was missing: 12 points better than last week.
  • Seven thin columns replace the stacked bar — one hue, light for the week, dark for today. A single series over time needs a ramp, not three status colours, so the contrast and CVD problems disappear with the categories. 134
  • Focus sessions, coins and the completion count survive as a quiet footer row. They're still there; they've stopped shouting. 67

What it costs

  • Needs seven days of per-day history. This is the only option that won't render from the current week-total payload.
  • Late and missed stop being separable at a glance — you'd get that on the "See all" screen instead.
  • The biggest departure from the approved comp of the three, visually.
3

Momentum — built for the audience

An audit card is a strange thing to show a teenager with ADHD at the end of a hard week. Same data, reframed around what's continuing rather than what was missed — and with the red taken out entirely.

What changes

  • Streak leads. It's the one number that rewards showing up tomorrow rather than grading yesterday.
  • Seven pips carry the same breakdown the stacked bar was mangling, but per day and by shape as well as colour — filled, ringed, dashed. Colour is never the only cue. 134
  • Coins become a meter against a real target, which is what makes 70 legible. 7
  • No red anywhere. A missed day is a hollow outline — visible, countable, not an alarm. 9

What it costs

  • Needs two things the payload may not have: a streak count and a reward threshold.
  • Softest of the three for a parent or coach who wants the audit — that view needs to stay on "See all".
  • A streak that breaks can sting. Worth a rule that it never displays 0 — show "start a new streak" instead.

If it were mine

Ship Corrected now — the overlapping segments and the invisible 17% are live bugs, and that version needs no new data or new conversation. Then run Momentum as the real candidate for the home screen, because the audit framing is the deeper problem and a streak is the cheapest thing that fixes it.

One story is the strongest card of the three in isolation, but it's a weekly-review screen wearing a widget's clothes. If the home screen only gets one card, it shouldn't be the one that drops the late-versus-missed distinction.
